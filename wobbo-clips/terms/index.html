1. What the service does

Wobbo Clips takes videos you send to its Telegram bot and automatically edits them into short vertical clips: it transcribes speech, trims pauses and filler words, reframes the video to 9:16, normalizes audio, and adds captions and a short quote. The finished clip is sent back to you in Telegram as a preview and uploaded to your TikTok inbox as a draft through TikTok's Content Posting API. You decide whether and how to publish it in the TikTok app. The service never publishes anything on your behalf.
