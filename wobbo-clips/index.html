Wobbo Clips

Your footage, ready for vertical video.

A small editing tool by Eugene Shemchuk.

Wobbo Clips turns videos you filmed yourself into short, captioned vertical clips and sends them to your TikTok drafts. You review and post them yourself.

How it works

  1. You send one or more of your own videos to the Wobbo Clips Telegram bot, optionally with a quote in the message caption.
  2. The video is processed automatically on our server: speech is transcribed, pauses and filler words are trimmed, the video is reframed to 9:16, audio levels are normalized, and word-by-word captions plus a motivational quote are added.
  3. You get a preview in Telegram, and the finished clip is uploaded to your TikTok inbox as a draft.
  4. You open TikTok, review the draft, edit the caption, choose who can see it, and publish it yourself. Nothing is ever posted automatically.

What it uses from TikTok

Wobbo Clips uses TikTok Login Kit to connect your account and the Content Posting API (video.upload) to send finished clips to your drafts. It does not read your videos, followers, messages or analytics.
