Privacy Policy

Effective 6 October 2026

This policy explains what data Wobbo Clips ("the service") handles and why. The service is operated by Eugene Shemchuk, Amsterdam, the Netherlands, who is the data controller.

Data we process

  • Videos you send, including the people and voices in them, and any text you add as a caption.
  • Data derived from your videos: a word-by-word transcript with timestamps, the edit plan, and the finished clip.
  • Telegram data: the chat ID and message metadata needed to receive your videos and send you previews and status messages.
  • TikTok data: the access and refresh tokens TikTok issues when you connect your account, your TikTok open ID and basic profile information (user.info.basic), and the upload ID TikTok returns for each draft.

We do not collect your TikTok videos, followers, messages, contacts or analytics, and the service has no ads, tracking cookies or analytics.

How we use it

Only to provide the service: to edit your videos into clips, send you previews, and upload finished clips to your own TikTok inbox when you use the service. The legal basis is performing the service you ask for (GDPR Art. 6(1)(b)). We never sell your data or use it for advertising or for training AI models.

Where it is processed

Videos are processed on a server we operate. Speech transcription runs on that same server (open-source Whisper model); your audio is not sent to any third-party AI or transcription service. Your data is shared only with:

  • Telegram, which delivers your videos and our replies (Telegram Privacy Policy).
  • TikTok, which receives the finished clip when it is uploaded to your drafts (TikTok Privacy Policy).
  • Our hosting provider, which stores the server's data on our behalf.

How long we keep it

  • Videos, transcripts and finished clips are deleted automatically 30 days after the last activity on that job (so a failed job can still be retried), or sooner if you ask. Copies of your uploads held by our Telegram file server are deleted on the same schedule.
  • TikTok tokens are kept until you disconnect the service or ask us to delete them. A TikTok refresh token expires after at most one year.
  • Telegram messages in your chat with the bot are stored by Telegram; you can delete them in Telegram at any time.

Security

Data is stored on a server with access limited to the operator, tokens are stored in a file readable only by the service, and all connections to Telegram and TikTok use HTTPS.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you can ask for access to, correction of, or deletion of your data, and object to or restrict its processing. You can disconnect TikTok at any time in the TikTok app (Settings and privacy, Security, Manage app permissions). You can also complain to the Dutch Data Protection Authority (Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens).

Children

The service is not intended for anyone under the minimum age required to use TikTok in their country.

Changes

If this policy changes, the effective date above is updated.
